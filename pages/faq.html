Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common StudentHub questions.

What is StudentHub?

StudentHub is a centralized student portal for academic information, events, notices, resources and student services.

How do I create an account?

Open the Register page, complete the required information and accept the terms and conditions.

How do I access my dashboard?

Use the Login page and then open the Dashboard from the navigation menu.

Can StudentHub be expanded later?

Yes. Backend, database, authentication and REST-style APIs can be added in future practicals.
