About the project

About StudentHub

StudentHub is a centralized web portal designed to simplify access to student information and services.

Our Mission

The goal of StudentHub is to provide students with one organized platform for academic information, notices, events, resources and student services.

Simple

Clear navigation helps students find information quickly.

Accessible

Semantic HTML and accessible form controls support different users.

Scalable

The project can later be connected to a backend, database and APIs.

Project Scope

The initial version focuses on the frontend structure, responsive UI, DOM interaction and client-side validation. Future practicals can extend the system with backend functionality and database integration.
